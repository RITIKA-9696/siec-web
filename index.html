Our Story

Building a Sustainable Future

SIEC Water is a specialized division of Sustainable Integrated Environmental Consultant (SIEC), dedicated exclusively to delivering innovative and sustainable water solutions. We focus on the design, development, and implementation of Water Treatment Plants (WTP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), and Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), with a strong commitment to environmental responsibility and advanced engineering practices.
We believe in empowering environmental progress through smart water systems, contributing to a future where water security, ecological balance, and sustainability go hand in hand.

OUR PROCESS

Our Project Execution Process

Every project follows a structured methodology from environmental assessment and engineering design to execution, commissioning, and long-term sustainability support.

1. Requirement Analysis

Comprehensive water/wastewater quality studies, flow assessment, and feasibility evaluation to define clear project goals.

2. Concept & Design

3. Engineering & Procurement

Selection and sourcing of world-class equipment, membranes, chemicals, and automation systems with strict quality checks.

4. Fabrication & Construction

Custom skid fabrication, tank construction, and civil integration executed with safety, precision, and adherence to timelines.

5. Installation & Commissioning
